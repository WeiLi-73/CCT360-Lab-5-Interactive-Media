About the Project & References

This project reflects on Suzhou, Jiangsu, China, through an imagined garden soundscape. The illustration depicts the fictional Grand View Garden, rather than a specific garden in Suzhou. The sourced recordings are used as sound design materials, not presented as recordings made in Suzhou.

  1. Sun Wen. Sun Wen Red Chamber Daguanyuan . Wikimedia Commons. Public domain.
  2. Charlie Huang. Liu Shui (Flowing Water) . Guqin performance, 2006. CC BY 2.5 . An excerpt was trimmed and mixed with environmental sounds.
  3. Fg2. Flowing-water-100019 . 2008. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. Repeated and edited for this composition.
  4. ezwa. Birds singing in garden . 2007. Recorded in a garden in Belgium. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. Edited for this composition.
